How to hunt

Drag from the first letter to the last. Words run in eight directions, including backwards. Select the complete word; overlaps are welcome.

Keyboard: Tab into the grid. Arrow keys move. Enter or Space starts a selection; arrows extend it; Enter or Space submits. Escape cancels.

Hints: a letter costs 1 coin; a start location costs 3. Hints never finish a word. You start with 25 coins. First completions earn 20, plus 10 without hints. Daily puzzles add 30 and a streak bonus of 2–14 coins. Replays earn no extra coins.

Your progress stays in this browser. No account, payments, or tracking. Sound is not used.
